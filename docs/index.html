03 / METHOD

From ratings to outcomes

Match predictions become inputs to repeated tournament simulations. The proportion of runs in which a team reaches each stage estimates its probability.

  1. 01

    Prepare team features

    EA Sports FIFA game ratings, Elo ratings, and FIFA ranking.

  2. 02

    Predict matches

    Compare Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost. The project reports XGBoost as the selected model.

  3. 03

    Simulate the tournament

    Resolve groups and knockout matches, record the champion, and repeat.
